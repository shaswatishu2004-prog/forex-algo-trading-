FINDING 01

Bar counts by series

106,394 bars

The ohlcv table holds 106,394 bars in six series. Minute rows dominate the file: the three 1m series add up to 79,788 bars against 26,606 daily bars, because one full day of minute data is about 1,400 rows while a full year of daily data is 252.

DXY carries the deepest daily history at 14,148 bars back to 1971-01-04. EURUSD stops at 5,918 bars from 2003-12-01, which is where its Yahoo series begins.

FINDING 02

Volume exists on one series

99.9% covered

Volume above zero appears on 99.915% of XAUUSD minute bars (25,776 of 25,798) and on 93.6% of its daily bars. EURUSD and DXY read zero at both timeframes, and DXY daily shows volume on 2 stray bars only.

The VWAP weight for the two spot series therefore comes from the synthetic formula (H-L) + |C_t - C_(t-1)|. That substitution is a fact about the data, not a preference, and the two weight schemes will not produce identical bands.

FINDING 03

Minute gaps follow the trading week

30 UTC dates

Grouped by UTC date the minute series are nearly complete on weekdays: EURUSD about 1,436, XAUUSD about 1,378, DXY about 1,339 bars on a typical weekday. All three series are empty on every Saturday in the window: Aug 29, Sep 05, Sep 12, and Sep 19. Sundays hold only the market open: 60 bars on EURUSD, 110 bars on XAUUSD, 120 bars on DXY as a typical value. XAUUSD alone is empty on Sep 07 while the other two symbols keep trading.

The first and last columns are window boundaries rather than session gaps. On 2026-08-24 only EURUSD holds bars (60). On 2026-09-22, XAUUSD holds 239 bars and DXY 237 while EURUSD holds none.

FINDING 04

VWAP bands on gold futures

real COMEX volume

Bands rebuilt from 4,016 one-minute bars over 2026-09-17 to 2026-09-21 with reported volume as the weight. The close stayed inside the 1 sigma band on 59.5% of bars and inside the 3 sigma band on 99.8%.

The anchor resets 3 times in the window, at UTC midnight, so every day starts with the bands collapsed on the first bar and widens them as volume accumulates.

FINDING 05

VWAP bands on EURUSD

synthetic weight

Same window and same formula, weight (H-L) + |C_t - C_(t-1)| because spot FX publishes no volume. The close stayed inside the 1 sigma band on 47.0% of bars and inside the 3 sigma band on 98.7%.

The synthetic weight reacts to range and price change instead of traded size, so EURUSD bands track price shape more closely than the volume weighted gold bands do. Both percentages describe one three day sample, not a proven hit rate.

FINDING 06

Regime labels on EURUSD daily bars

780 bars charted

Labels computed over the full daily history and charted across the last 780 bars from 2023-09-20. Over that window the four states split 25% uptrend, 18% downtrend, 23% high volatility, 33% low volatility.

Priority shapes the result: 123 bars (27% of the window) carry trend evidence yet print high volatility, because volatility beats trend by design. The efficiency ratio fallback supplies 146 labels when ADX reads below 25.

high volatilityuptrendlow volatilitydowntrend
Every bar carries exactly one label, so a strong trend inside high volatility reads as high volatility rather than as uptrend. Two independent axes would be a different design.

FINDING 07

Daily history dwarfs minute history

30 dates of 1m

Drawn to one scale, daily bars reach 1971-01-04, a span of 56 years, while minute bars cover 30 UTC dates. On that scale the minute window is 0.8 pixels wide, which is why the second panel zooms into it.

The zoomed panel is the real constraint: a minute level backtest runs on 28 days of data today, and the deeper HistData download has to land before any minute level claim is credible.

FINDING 08

Timestamps store three offsets

mixed timezone

The timestamp column keeps the source offset and the source format: +0100 on EURUSD minute bars, -0400 on XAUUSD and DXY minute bars, and +00:00 on daily bars. One UTC instant therefore writes three different local strings, and minute rows carry no colon while daily rows do.

A SQL range filter on the raw text sorts those strings wrongly, so every timestamp has to be parsed to UTC before any comparison. The chart shows where each stored 24 hour window sits against the UTC clock.
