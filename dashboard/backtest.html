VERDICT

What this run says

unprofitable
  • Costs eat the whole edge and moreGross +0.85% against +82.77% of round-trip costs over 6,687 trades. Spread alone is +41.14%.
  • The hit rate is a genuine strategy result, not a session artifact32.4% of exits land on the target. Stops fire far more often than targets, and excluding the session-boundary exits does not move the number.
  • Stress at twice the costs is worse still-134.79% net. A strategy that needs kinder costs to break even is not robust.
  • The daily loss halt still stops entries inside a bad day127 halts. The -81.92% drawdown limit was breached and is recorded rather than enforced, so the year runs to completion.
  • Locked final test held out1,152 trades in the last block, untouched during development. Regime filters rejected 17,055 signals that the trend, volatility, and direction rules did not allow.
