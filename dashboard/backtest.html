VERDICT

What this run says

unprofitable
  • Costs eat the whole edge and moreGross +0.83% against +44.73% of round-trip costs over 2,302 trades. Spread alone is +20.48%.
  • The two structural filters cut the loss in half but moved no edgeDropping Asia and any target worth under 3x the round trip took the run from 6,687 trades and -81.92% net to 2,302 trades and -43.90% net, with costs down from +82.77% to +44.73%. Gross barely moved, +0.85% before against +0.83% now, so the removed trades carried cost and no edge. Cheaper trading cannot rescue a signal that earns nothing before costs.
  • The hit rate is a genuine strategy result, not a session artifact44.0% of exits land on the target. Stops fire far more often than targets, and excluding the session-boundary exits does not move the number.
  • Stress at twice the costs is worse still-67.03% net. A strategy that needs kinder costs to break even is not robust.
  • The daily loss halt still stops entries inside a bad day46 halts. The 15% portfolio drawdown limit was breached on 2025-12-30 and is recorded rather than enforced, so the year runs to completion.
  • Locked final test held out403 trades in the last block, untouched during development. Regime filters rejected 17,055 signals that the trend, volatility, and direction rules did not allow.
