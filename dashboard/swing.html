SCOPE

What this variant trades

donchian_20d_swing_v1
  • Signaldonchian_20d_swing_v1: a 20-day Donchian channel measured over bars t-20 to t-1, so the window excludes the signal bar itself.
  • EntriesDecided at the daily bar close, filled at the next daily open — the 21:00 UTC roll; a weekend signal fills at Sunday's roll.
  • ExitsA ratcheted chandelier trail that never loosens, an opposite-regime flip filled at the next open, a 20-session hold cap, and a forced close at the window edge.
  • SessionsEvery fill books on the roll, so all 24 trades land in the asia session bucket; the flat spread model understates roll spreads and the 2x stress is the partial mitigation.
  • Fill delayEntries fill 1 bar after the signal; the sensitivity panel shows the two-bar variant.

Swing variant: daily bars over a trailing year, so Sharpe and Sortino rest on roughly 250 equity points; they are not comparable to the one-minute report and say little about tails.
