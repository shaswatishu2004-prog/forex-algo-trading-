ACTIVE STRATEGY

Institutional VWAP bands

1m timeframe
  • Anchored VWAPTypical price (H+L+C)/3 weighted across the anchor window and recomputed every minute.
  • 1-sigma and 3-sigma bandsEach band plots VWAP plus or minus one and three weighted standard deviations, reset with the anchor.
  • Volume weightsGold futures use reported COMEX volume. EURUSD and DXY use a synthetic activity weight, because spot FX and the index publish no volume.
  • Anchor window21:00 UTC session reset every day, the institutional convention, resolved in the anchor decision.
VWAP = sum(w * p) / sum(w) sigma = sqrt(sum(w * (p - VWAP)^2) / sum(w)) bands = VWAP +/- {1 sigma, 3 sigma}

Daily bars carry the regime label, 1-minute bars carry the bands, where microstructure cost matters most. Formulas and sources live in vwap-regime-research.md.
